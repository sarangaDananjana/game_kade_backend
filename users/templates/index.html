The Ecosystem

A three-party model built for institutional scale.

Meal Drop removes the complexity of institutional catering by decentralizing supply through vetted local cooks, using existing canteen infrastructure for last-metre distribution.

Quality Standards

Institutional-grade food safety at every stage of the chain.

Meal Drop operates a non-negotiable quality framework. Every cook is certified and audited. Every meal is sealed, labelled, and temperature-tracked. Institutional partners require nothing less.

View Our Standards Download Compliance Report

Cook Vetting

Every cook undergoes kitchen inspection, food handling certification, and identity verification before onboarding.

Tamper-Proof Sealing

All meal containers are sealed with QR-coded tamper seals, verifiable by the canteen and consumer at pickup.

Temperature Logging

Meals are prepared within a 3-hour dispatch window with insulated transport to preserve safe temperatures.

Monthly Audits

Operational audits are conducted monthly at every canteen hub, with compliance scores published quarterly.
